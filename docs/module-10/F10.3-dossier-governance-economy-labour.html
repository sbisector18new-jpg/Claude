IMPORTANT · THE WINDOW, THE CUT-OFF, AND WHAT THAT MEANS

This chapter and F10.4 together are the exhaustive twelve-month dossier: events from August 2025 to the cut-off of 1 August 2026. Every item carries a source link.

Three conventions govern the whole dossier, and they matter more than any single fact in it.

1 The cut-off is applied strictly. Where an event straddles 1 August 2026 it is included and the straddle is stated — the Commonwealth Games closed on 2 August 2026, one day outside, and F10.4 §5 says so rather than quietly rounding. Where an item falls days before the window opens it is marked as a preceding anchor, as NISAR's launch of 30 July 2025 is in F10.4 §3.

2 Weighting follows F10.1 §1.2, not the news cycle. An EPFO paper's eight current affairs marks are not distributed like a general knowledge quiz. §1 — labour, social security and the EPFO — is the longest section in the dossier and should be read first and twice. Sport, awards and obituaries are compact by deliberate decision, and F10.4 §5 records why.

3 The dossier does not replace F10.1's system; it starts it. F10.1 §3 prescribes a monthly register the candidate keeps himself. This chapter supplies the twelve months already elapsed, so that the register begins with a full year behind it rather than from zero. From today, you maintain it. F10.1 §4's twelve-item sweep still applies to every figure here.

1.2 What the final rules did, and did not, do

ItemPosition
Minimum wage calculation formulaDropped from the final rules
Floor wageProvided for, to be fixed on minimum living standards; the rules do not specify detailed norms
National floor level minimum wageRemains advisory, not binding — about ₹176 to ₹178 a day, unrevised since 2017–2019
Definition of wagesBasic pay plus dearness allowance must be at least 50% of total remuneration; any excess above 50% is added back to wages

The 50% rule is the provision with the largest practical effect, and it is the one F10.2 §1.2 identifies as the change that most affects EPFO administration: it prevents an employer from structuring pay to keep the wage component — and therefore the provident fund contribution — artificially small.

HIGH YIELD · EPFO OFFICERS, INCLUDING APFCs, NOTIFIED UNDER THE SOCIAL SECURITY CODE

This is the single most directly relevant item in the entire dossier to the post you are applying for.

The Government has notified EPFO officers as authorities under Chapter III of the Code on Social Security, 2020, to operationalise enforcement, recovery and compliance under the Code. The categories named are:

  • Central Provident Fund Commissioners
  • Additional Central Provident Fund Commissioners
  • Regional Provident Fund Commissioners
  • Assistant Provident Fund Commissioners
  • Enforcement Officers

They have been appointed as Recovery Officers for the recovery of dues and enforcement of provident fund obligations under the Code.

Source: Taxmann on the notification of EPFO officers under the Code

Why this matters twice over. First, it is an obviously examinable fact about the organisation. Second, and more usefully, it tells you what the job now is: the statutory powers an Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner exercises — inquiry, determination, recovery — now flow from the Code on Social Security, 2020 rather than from the 1952 Act directly, because the 1952 Act has been subsumed into it (F10.2 §1.2).

For the interview, that is a far better answer than reciting Section 7A: the powers are the same in substance, but their source has moved, and the officer's authority now sits inside a consolidated code covering provident fund, insurance, gratuity, maternity benefit and gig workers together. Content rephrased from the source for compliance with licensing restrictions.

1.5 The India–UK Double Contributions Convention

HIGH YIELD · A SOCIAL SECURITY AGREEMENT THE EPFO ITSELF ADMINISTERS

ItemPosition
What it isA Double Contributions Convention (DCC) — a type of Social Security Agreement coordinating the payment of contributions
In force15 July 2026, simultaneously with the India–UK trade agreement
What it doesPrevents double social security contributions for workers temporarily posted between the two countries
Detachment exemptionUp to 60 months — five years
MechanismA certificate of coverage: the posted employee remains under home country legislation and is exempt in the host country
What it does NOT doIt does not govern access to benefits such as the State Pension
EPFO's roleEPFO has issued the procedure for claiming exemption

Sources: UK Government DCC explainer · Ministry of Commerce note on CETA and the DCC entering into force · Business Today on the EPFO's exemption procedure and the 60-month limit

Why an APFC candidate should know this cold. Social Security Agreements are administered through the EPFO, which issues certificates of coverage to Indian workers going abroad and processes exemption claims for foreign nationals working in India. The DCC is the newest and largest such agreement, and the 60-month exemption is its headline number.

The distinction to be precise about: a DCC coordinates contributions, not benefits. An option saying it gives Indian workers access to the UK State Pension is wrong. Content rephrased from the sources for compliance with licensing restrictions.

TRAP · FOREX RESERVES ARE THE FASTEST-DECAYING FIGURE IN THIS MANUAL

The reserve figure is published weekly and moved by more than US$ 14 billion in a single week inside this window. Any specific number you memorise will be stale within a fortnight.

Learn the shape instead: reserves are in the region of US$ 690 to 710 billion, comprise foreign currency assets (the largest component), gold, SDRs and the reserve tranche position, and gold has been the main driver of recent increases.

That is what survives. The exact figure is a sweep item — F10.1 §4. Content rephrased from the sources for compliance with licensing restrictions.

KNOW · HOW TO REVISE A SCHEME LIST WITHOUT MEMORISING EVERY RUPEE

There are more schemes here than are worth learning to the last crore, and F10.1 §1.2's test applies: an item is worth learning if it attaches to something.

Learn these four, because each has a distinctive, single, memorable number:

  • PM-SSY — 5,000 MW, floating solar. The word Sarovar means lake, which is the mnemonic
  • Semicon 2.0 — ₹1.27 lakh crore, the largest single outlay in the list
  • MPMS — ₹62,500 crore
  • Khelo India revamp — ₹36,441 crore

And note the one conceptual point, which is likelier than any figure: PM-SSY is the first Indian scheme to pair floating solar with mandatory co-located storage, which addresses the intermittency problem rather than only capacity. That is the kind of statement an interview rewards and a which-of-the-following-is-not-correct item tests. Content rephrased from the sources for compliance with licensing restrictions.

THINK LIKE UPSC · THE FOUR SHAPES IN THIS BANK

  1. Two dates for one instrument. Q1, Q5, Q13, Q20, Q23, Q26, Q33. Seven of forty questions turn on distinguishing enactment from commencement, signature from entry into force, or election from oath. When a reform is recent, its dates cluster — and the cluster is the question
  2. The superseded figure. Q12 (100 » 125 days), Q16 (74% » 100%). In both cases the wrong option is what every book printed before 2026 says
  3. The general rule and its carve-out. Q18. Insurance FDI is 100% except LIC at 20%. Where a limit has an exception, expect to be asked about the exception
  4. The right number, the wrong scheme. Q38, Q39, Q40. ₹1.27 lakh crore, ₹62,500 crore, ₹36,441 crore, ₹99,446 crore, 5,000 MW, 10,000 MWh. Learn scheme, number and unit as a triple — F10.2 §4.2's rule
