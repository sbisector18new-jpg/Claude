**214 pages, 230 questions, and every format the 2023 paper used** — 10 vocabulary, 5 para jumbles, 5
comprehension on one passage, and 15 Quant across arithmetic, mensuration, algebra, probability and data
interpretation.

**This module is not read, it is run.** Thirty minutes an evening, in parallel with whatever module is
running in the morning, on F8.0's calendar. Do not wait until you have read the eight chapters — go to
**F8.0 §3, week 1, Monday** and start tomorrow, then read each chapter as the calendar reaches it.

Depth follows the evidence rather than being spread evenly. **F8.5 has the largest bank** because
arithmetic supplied more 2023 items than any other Quant topic, and **F8.6 has the most known previous
questions** — the hemisphere, the melted disc, circle geometry and heights and distances were all asked.
**Grammar (F8.4) was not separately tested at all in 2023** and is written to be finished in two evenings,
because its value is in protecting the vocabulary and para-jumble marks rather than in earning its own.

**Reasoning is deliberately one session of one chapter.** The 2023 paper contained **exactly one pure
reasoning question in 120**. Every commercial book for this exam devotes hundreds of pages to syllogisms,
blood relations and seating arrangements; on the available evidence that is a very poor use of your hours.
F8.8 §5 gives you the seven patterns that recover most of the value from a single question in about forty
minutes of reading, and the Master Content Register records the decision so it is not silently reversed.

## Reading order, and the shortcuts

**Module 8 runs in parallel with everything else** — it is the evening 30 minutes, from week 1, and it is
never a block. The rest is sequential.

Within Module 6, read **F6.1 » F6.2 » F6.3 » F6.4** in order; each depends on the one before. F6.5 to F7.1
are independent and can be taken in any order. Module 9's chapters are independent of each other.

Within Module 8, the chapters are independent, but **F8.0 comes first** and the calendar interleaves the
rest deliberately — English one evening, Quant the next.

If your time collapses, these are the sections that carry the most marks:

- **F8.0 §4.1** — the fraction, decimal and percentage equivalents. The single highest-value page in the
  Quant half: it converts most percentage questions into one-step multiplications
- **F8.6 §2.2** — the hemisphere's three surface areas, and which one a question is asking for
- **F8.7 §2** — the sum and product of the roots of a quadratic. Most questions about roots never require
  you to find them
- **F8.8 §4.1** — percentage against percentage point, the most examined distinction in data interpretation
- **F8.1 §5** — the word list, ten a day, every day. Half the English block
- **F6.1 §6** — trial balance and the four errors it cannot catch
- **F6.2 §5** — capital versus revenue, and the five one-word pairs
- **F6.3 §5 and §6** — the year-end adjustments, and provision versus reserve
- **F6.4 §2 and §5** — SLM versus WDV, and AS 2's lower of cost and net realisable value
- **F6.7 §2** — the scope of audit, the only section in Module 6 with direct PYQ evidence

## Rebuilding

```
python3 build/build.py                        # src/*.md  ->  docs/*.html
python3 build/check.py                        # quality gate — must pass before commit
env -u NODE_OPTIONS node build/pdf.mjs        # docs/*.html -> pdf/*.pdf
```

`env -u NODE_OPTIONS` is not optional: the environment presets a `--require` hook pointing at a file that
does not exist, so every plain `node` call dies with `MODULE_NOT_FOUND`.

Sources in `src/` are the master manuscript. `docs/` and `pdf/` are generated — never edit them by hand.
