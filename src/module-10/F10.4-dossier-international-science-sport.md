## 1. Session 1 — International Relations and Summits ★★★★☆

### 1.1 COP30, Belém

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| **Venue and dates** | **Belém, Brazil**, **10 to 21 November 2025**, concluding **22 November** |
| **Principal decision** | The **"Global Mutirão"** decision — *mutirão* being a Brazilian term for collective effort |
| Occasion | The **tenth anniversary** of the **Paris Agreement** |
| **Finance** | Agreement calling for a **tripling of funding** for developing countries to adapt to climate impacts |
| **Fossil fuels** | **No clear commitment to transition away from fossil fuels in the main decision** — the principal criticism of the outcome |
| Separate instrument | A **"Belém Declaration"**, led by **Colombia**, in which a coalition of countries committed to a just and orderly transition away from fossil fuels |
| Other outcomes | **Global indicators for adaptation**; a **just transition mechanism**; a new **Gender Action Plan**; a three-year dialogue on trade |
| Context | 2024 confirmed as the warmest year on record, about **1.55 °C** above pre-industrial levels |

Sources: [United Nations on the COP30 outcome](https://www.un.org/en/climatechange/cop30) ·
[UN News on the finance package and the absence of a fossil fuel commitment](https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/11/1166433) ·
[IISD summary of the negotiations](https://enb.iisd.org/belem-un-climate-change-conference-cop30-summary)

::: trap | TRAP · THE MUTIRÃO DECISION AND THE BELÉM DECLARATION ARE DIFFERENT THINGS
This is the most likely confusion in the whole section, and it is a clean four-option question.

- The **Global Mutirão decision** is the **formal COP30 outcome**, adopted by the conference. It contains the
  finance package — and **does not** commit parties to move away from fossil fuels
- The **Belém Declaration** is a **separate, voluntary political declaration** led by **Colombia**, signed by
  a coalition rather than by the COP, which **does** address transition away from fossil fuels

**So an option stating that COP30 agreed to phase out fossil fuels is wrong**, and an option stating that no
country committed to it is also wrong. The distinction is between what the conference decided and what a
coalition declared alongside it. *Content rephrased from the sources for compliance with licensing
restrictions.*
:::

## 3. Session 3 — Science, Space and Technology ★★★★☆

### 3.1 NISAR — the preceding anchor

::: know | KNOW · LAUNCHED 30 JULY 2025, TWO DAYS BEFORE THE WINDOW OPENS
**NISAR** — the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar — was launched on **30 July 2025** aboard **GSLV-F16**.
That is **two days before this dossier's window begins**, and it is included because it is the most
significant Indian space event adjacent to the period and because a paper set in 2026 may still ask about
it.

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| **Significance** | The **first joint ISRO–NASA satellite mission** |
| **Technical first** | The **first dual-frequency (L-band and S-band) radar** Earth observation satellite |
| Configuration | NASA's **12-metre unfurlable antenna** integrated on an **Indian satellite bus**, launched on an **Indian launch vehicle** |
| Purpose | Global microwave imaging using **fully polarimetric and interferometric** data |

Sources: [PIB on NISAR's dual-frequency radar](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2298798) ·
[ISRO on the mission as a first joint ISRO-NASA venture](https://www.isro.gov.in/Achievements_Department_of_Space_2025.html)

**Two things are asked about NISAR:** that it is **joint ISRO–NASA**, and that it is **dual-frequency L and
S band**. *Content rephrased from the sources for compliance with licensing restrictions.*

::: know | KNOW · THE TWO THAT CONNECT TO MODULE 9, AND THE ONE THAT CONNECTS TO MODULE 4
Nobel questions are usually pure recall, but three of these attach to the static syllabus, which per F10.1
§1.2 is what makes them worth learning:

- **Chemistry — metal-organic frameworks** are porous crystalline materials with very large internal surface
  area, used for gas storage and separation. This is F9.3's chemistry
- **Physics — macroscopic quantum tunnelling** in a superconducting circuit is the physical basis of
  superconducting **qubits**, and therefore of quantum computing. F9.2 and F9.5
- **Economics — innovation-driven growth** is endogenous growth theory, which is the theoretical answer to
  F4.1 §3.2's distinction between growth and development

**László Krasznahorkai is the second Hungarian laureate in Literature**, after Imre Kertész in 2002.
